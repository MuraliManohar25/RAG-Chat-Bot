COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY
HOSTEL GUIDELINES AND FEE STRUCTURE 2026

Chapter 1: Hostel Accommodation

1.1 Eligibility
Hostel accommodation is available for all enrolled students on a first-come, first-served basis. Priority is given to outstation students.

1.2 Room Types
- Single Occupancy: Limited availability
- Double Occupancy: Standard rooms with attached/common bathrooms
- Triple Occupancy: Economy rooms

Chapter 2: Hostel Fee Structure (Academic Year 2026-2027)

2.1 Annual Hostel Fees
Boys Hostel:
- Double Occupancy: Rs. 45,000 per year
- Triple Occupancy: Rs. 35,000 per year

Girls Hostel:
- Double Occupancy: Rs. 48,000 per year
- Triple Occupancy: Rs. 38,000 per year

2.2 Additional Charges
- Mess Fee: Rs. 3,500 per month (compulsory)
- Electricity charges: As per actual consumption
- Caution Deposit: Rs. 5,000 (refundable)

2.3 Payment Schedule
Hostel fees must be paid in two installments:
- First installment: Before commencement of semester
- Second installment: Before start of even semester

Chapter 3: Hostel Rules

3.1 Timings
Hostel gate closes at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:00 PM on weekends.

3.2 Visitors
Visitors are allowed only in the common areas between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM with prior permission.

3.3 Prohibited Items
Cooking appliances, illegal substances, and pets are strictly prohibited in hostel premises.
