COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY
ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026

Chapter 1: General Provisions

1.1 Purpose
These academic regulations govern all undergraduate and postgraduate programs offered by the College of Engineering and Technology for the academic year 2026-2027.

Chapter 2: Attendance Requirements

2.1 Minimum Attendance
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each course to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Attendance is calculated based on the total number of classes conducted during the semester.

2.2 Consequences of Low Attendance
Students below the required attendance threshold may be subject to academic restrictions, including denial of hall ticket for end-semester examinations. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation as per university norms.

2.3 Medical Leave
Medical leave up to 15 days per semester may be considered for attendance calculation upon submission of valid medical certificates from registered medical practitioners.

Chapter 3: Examination Rules

3.1 End-Semester Examinations
End-semester examinations are conducted at the end of each semester. Students must carry their identity cards and hall tickets to the examination hall.

3.2 Internal Assessment
Internal assessment carries 40% weightage and includes mid-semester tests, assignments, and laboratory evaluations.

3.3 Malpractice
Any form of examination malpractice will result in disciplinary action as per university regulations, including possible cancellation of the examination.

Chapter 4: Grading System

4.1 Grade Points
The college follows a 10-point grading system. A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required for progression to the next semester.

4.2 Backlog Rules
Students with backlogs must clear them within the stipulated time frame as per university regulations.

Chapter 5: Academic Calendar 2026-2027

5.1 Odd Semester
Semester begins: July 15, 2026
Mid-semester examinations: September 20-25, 2026
End-semester examinations: November 25 - December 10, 2026

5.2 Even Semester
Semester begins: January 10, 2027
Mid-semester examinations: March 15-20, 2027
End-semester examinations: May 10 - May 25, 2027
