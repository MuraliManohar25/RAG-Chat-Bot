COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY
ADMISSION PROSPECTUS 2026

Chapter 1: Undergraduate Admissions

1.1 Eligibility
Candidates must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with a minimum aggregate of 60% marks.

1.2 Required Documents
- 10th Mark Sheet and Certificate
- 12th Mark Sheet and Certificate
- Transfer Certificate from previous institution
- Migration Certificate (if applicable)
- Caste Certificate (if applicable)
- Income Certificate (for scholarship applicants)
- Aadhaar Card copy
- Passport size photographs (6 copies)
- Medical fitness certificate

1.3 Admission Process
Step 1: Online application through college portal
Step 2: Document verification at admission office
Step 3: Fee payment and seat confirmation
Step 4: Orientation program attendance

Chapter 2: Fee Structure 2026-2027

2.1 Tuition Fees (Per Year)
Computer Science & Engineering: Rs. 1,25,000
Mechanical Engineering: Rs. 1,10,000
Electrical Engineering: Rs. 1,10,000
Electronics & Communication: Rs. 1,15,000
Civil Engineering: Rs. 1,00,000
Business Administration: Rs. 90,000

2.2 Other Fees
- Development Fee: Rs. 15,000 (one-time)
- Library Fee: Rs. 3,000 per year
- Laboratory Fee: Rs. 5,000 per year
- Examination Fee: As per university norms

Chapter 3: Scholarships

3.1 Merit Scholarship
Top 10% students based on entrance rank receive 50% tuition fee waiver.

3.2 Need-Based Scholarship
Students from economically weaker sections (family income below Rs. 2.5 lakhs) may apply for fee concessions.

3.3 Sports Scholarship
State and national level sports achievers are eligible for partial fee waiver.
