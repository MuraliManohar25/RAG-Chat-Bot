COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY
SCHOLARSHIP AND PLACEMENT GUIDE 2026

PART A: SCHOLARSHIPS

A.1 Institutional Scholarships
Merit Scholarship: 50% tuition waiver for top 10% entrance rank holders.
Sports Excellence: 25-50% fee waiver for state/national sports medalists.
Cultural Excellence: 25% fee waiver for recognized cultural achievements.

A.2 Government Scholarships
Students may apply for state and central government scholarships including:
- Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST students
- Merit-cum-Means Scholarship
- Pragati Scholarship for girl students

A.3 Application Deadline
Scholarship applications must be submitted by August 31, 2026.

PART B: PLACEMENTS

B.1 Training and Placement Cell
The Training and Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment, internships, and career guidance for all eligible students.

B.2 Eligibility for Campus Placement
- Minimum CGPA of 6.0
- No active backlogs
- Minimum 75% attendance in final year
- Completion of mandatory placement training modules
